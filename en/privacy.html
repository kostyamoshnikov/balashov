Privacy

A short note on what happens to the data you leave

What we collect

Only what booking needs

Data controller: Nikolai Alexandrovich Balashov, private individual.

The site and the Telegram bot collect data only when you submit an enquiry yourself: your name, event date, format, contact (phone or e-mail) and any details you write.

The form on the Booking page drafts an e-mail in your own mail client — sending isn't automatic, and nothing is stored on the site's server. The Telegram bot forwards your enquiry to the organiser (Nikolai) as a direct message or working chat — data goes nowhere else and isn't stored in any bot database.

The site doesn't use tracking cookies and doesn't share data with third parties, except where visit statistics are enabled (Yandex Metrica — see below).

Visit statistics

Yandex Metrica

If the Yandex Metrica counter is enabled on the site, it collects anonymised visit statistics (which pages are viewed, referral source) under Yandex's standard rules. The counter doesn't link this data to the name or contact details you leave in the booking form.

You can opt out via your browser settings (blocking JavaScript trackers or using a "Do Not Track" extension).

Your rights

Ask us to check or delete your data

To find out what data is held about you, or to request its deletion, write to booking@balashov-circus.ru — we'll respond within a reasonable time.
