Technical rider · in brief

What the act needs

  • Clear space for the free-standing ladder and stilts — ceiling/canopy height from 4.5 m
  • A level, firm surface for equilibristics
  • Permission and space for open-flame work (for the stilts act) — agreed separately with the venue
  • For the flexible-pole and tube acts — we bring our own equipment

Exact figures (load, power, load-in timing) are in the full rider, available on request once the format is confirmed.

Flexible poles · technical details

What to know when booking

  • 2 poles in stock — 6 m tall, flexing up to 2.5 m to each side, rotating around their axis
  • Total equipment weight — 120 kg
  • Rigging both poles takes about 1 h 40 min
  • Minimum ceiling height for installation — from 9 m (works in shopping malls too)
  • Works year-round, indoors and outdoors — an unusual attraction for Russia
  • Up to 5 working sets a day; no more than two sets during an extra rehearsal
  • For out-of-town events, the organiser covers three meals a day, accommodation and a warm waiting area

Equipment rental and gymnasts' fees are available on request.
