Format

One act — or a whole show

Every event has Nikolai himself on it — forty years in the ring, every performance under his name and his responsibility.

For larger formats, he assembles the right team. That's how the circus troupe for "Princess of the Circus" at the Musical Comedy Theatre came together, and for the paramotor flying show he brought in a champion pilot and four trapeze gymnasts.

"Shou Balashova" spans everything from a single act at a wedding to a full production at a city festival — and either way, Nikolai is the one on site.

Russian Book of Records · 2009

The most fragile balance

An officially registered Russian record in the category "Most Fragile Balance in Russia" — it started with balancing on three plastic cups and equilibrium on rolls.
